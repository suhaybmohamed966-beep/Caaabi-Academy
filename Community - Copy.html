FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Wax kasta oo aad rabto inaad ka ogaato mentorship-ka.

Waa session gaar ah oo adiga iyo mentor-ka oo keliya ah. Waxaad ka hadli kartaa skill, project, career ama dhibaato gaar ah oo aad rabto inaad xalliso.

Isticmaal search-ka iyo category filters-ka. Eeg mentor-ka expertise, rating, availability iyo session pricing ka hor intaadan booking samayn.

Qiimuhu wuxuu ku xiran yahay mentor-ka. Mentor kasta wuxuu leeyahay session price u gaar ah, waxaana ka hor arki kartaa card-kiisa.

Diyaari su'aalahaaga, project-kaaga ama hadafka aad rabto inaad gaarto. Haddii aad leedahay code ama design aad rabto feedback, waxaad mentor-ka la wadaagi kartaa inta session-ka lagu jiro.

Haa. Booking system-kaaga waxaad mustaqbalka ku dari kartaa cancellation iyo rescheduling policies si ay ugu shaqeeyaan mentor iyo learner labadaba.
